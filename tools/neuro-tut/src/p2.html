LOCALIZATION APPROACH: ที่ไหน (anatomy) + อะไร (etiology)

Weakness – possible manifestationsp.15

  • “Hemiparesis” almost always = CNS lesion (corticospinal tract, contralateral) – above/at cervical cord: cortex, subcortical, brainstem (cervical cord พบน้อย)
  • PNS (nerve) ทำ hemiparesis ไม่ได้
  • Quadriparesis: muscle · NMJ · nerve/root · ant. horn · cord→brain (ดูตารางหน้า 1)
  • Paraparesis: spinal cord (T/L/conus), early myopathy, polyneuropathy, parasagittal brain (ACA)
  • Monoparesis · Focal weakness

Pyramidal weakness patternp.14, 18

  • Upper limb: extensors weaker than flexors · Lower limb: flexors weaker than extensors → แขนงอ ขาเหยียด (Wernicke–Mann, circumduction gait)
  • Cortical / superficial corona radiata lesion → อ่อนแรงแขน-ขาไม่เท่ากัน (fiber กระจาย) · Internal capsule & brainstem → แขน = ขา (fiber รวมกัน)
  • Corticobulbar → facial nucleus: UMN facial palsy = lower face contralateral, forehead spared

AUTONOMIC · BLADDER

ANS pathwaysp.14

  • Sympathetic = thoracolumbar (T1–L2): pupil dilator, Müller m., sweat, vessels → Horner: ptosis, miosis, anhidrosis
  • Parasympathetic = craniosacral: EWN (CN3) → ciliary g. → pupil/ciliary m. · SSN (CN7) → PPG (lacrimal, nasal) & SMG (submandibular, sublingual) · ISN (CN9) → otic g. → parotid · DMN/NA (CN10) → organs · S2–4 → distal colon, bladder
  • Hypothalamus = main ANS control

Sensory examinationp.15

  • Large fiber (DCML): joint proprioception, vibration (tuning fork 128 Hz) · Small fiber (STT): pinprick, temperature
  • Romberg test = proprioception (balance ต้องใช้ 2 ใน 3: vision, proprioception, vestibular/cerebellum)

Gait · Cortical signs · Meningeal signsp.15

NEUROLOGICAL INVESTIGATIONS

เลือก investigationp.16

  • Head trauma + LOC → CT brain (acute bleed)
  • Sudden hemiparesis 90 min, CT ปกติ → MRI DWI (early ischemia)
  • Fever + headache + stiff neck, no focal → LP (CT ก่อนถ้ามี contraindication)
  • Tools: imaging, LP/CSF, EEG, NCS/RNS/EMG, blood (immune, neurodegenerative biomarkers)

Lumbar puncturep.16, 75

Skin→SubQ→Supraspinous lig.→Interspinous lig.→Ligamentum flavum (last before epidural/spinal canal)→Dura→Arachnoid → CSF
  • Indications Dx: meningitis (infectious, carcinomatous), SAH (ถ้า imaging ไม่ชัด), biomarkers (AD), CJD · Rx: CSF drainage (IIH, NPH), intrathecal drug
  • Contraindications: risk of brain herniation (focal deficit, ↑ICP, mass/midline shift, 4th ventricle outlet obstruction), skin infection, coagulopathy, cardiorespiratory compromise
  • CT before LP: focal deficit, ↑ICP/papilledema, ↓consciousness, immunocompromised, new seizure, suspected SOL
  • Complications: post-LP headache (most common), brain herniation (most serious), local pain, bleeding, infection, epidermoid cyst

CSF normal & SAHp.16, 68

  • OP 8–18 cmH₂O (↑ใน obese) · no RBC · WBC <5 (mononuclear) · protein 15–45 mg/dL · glucose 45–70 or 40–60% of blood
  • SAH: เลือดเท่ากันทุกหลอด + xanthochromia (pink oxyHb, yellow bilirubin; หลังเลือดออก 6–12 h) · Traumatic tap: เลือดจางลงตามหลอด
  • Xanthochromia อื่น: severe hyperbilirubinemia, CSF protein สูงมาก, metastatic melanoma

EEG – benefitsp.16

  • Diagnose seizure (ถ้าไม่แน่ใจ), classify seizure, localize focus, sleep events, monitor during carotid surgery, prognosis in coma, confirm brain death

Neuroimaging basicsp.46

  • CT: hyperdense = blood, calcification, contrast · hypodense = air, fat, CSF, edema · ดู symmetry, G-W differentiation, midline shift
  • T1: bright = fat, methemoglobin, slow flow, gadolinium · T2: bright = water (CSF, edema, cyst) · FLAIR: CSF ดำ, lesion ขาว (periventricular) · T2*/GRE: hemorrhage, Ca²⁺ · DWI: acute infarct ภายในนาที, abscess
  • Extra-axial signs: CSF cleft, displaced SA vessels, gray matter ระหว่าง mass กับ WM, broad dural base (dural tail), hyperostosis
  • Enhancement: pachymeningeal (meningioma, dural mets, post-op, intracranial hypotension) · leptomeningeal (meningitis, leptomeningeal mets) · gyriform (subacute infarct) · nodular at G-W jn (mets) · smooth ring = abscess · irregular necrotic ring = GBM
  • Hydrocephalus: communicating (absorption) vs non-communicating (obstruction proximal to Luschka/Magendie)
ALTERATION OF CONSCIOUSNESS · BRAIN DEATH · SYNCOPE

Consciousness = Arousal × Awarenessp.16

  • Arousal (level) ← ARAS (reticular formation midbrain → thalamus → diffuse cortex) · Awareness (content) ← cerebral cortex
  • Coma (ไม่มีทั้งคู่) · Vegetative state (arousal + no awareness) · Delirium (acute confusion, fluctuate)
  • ↓LOC ต้องเป็น bilateral cortex/diencephalon หรือ brainstem ARAS · unilateral frontal mass ไม่มี shift → ไม่ทำให้ซึม

Brain deathp.16

  • Coma จาก irreversible structural damage; exclude reversible: intoxication, severe hypothermia, endocrine/metabolic, shock
  • Absent ALL brainstem reflexes (pupil, corneal, VOR/doll’s, gag/cough) & no spontaneous movement ≥ 6 h
  • Positive apnea test · ประเมินโดยแพทย์ ≥ 3 คน

Syncopep.16

  • Transient global hypoperfusion (ARAS/cortex) → TLOC วินาที (max 1–2 min), recovery เร็ว ไม่มี deficit
  • 3 types: reflex (vasovagal) · orthostatic (dehydration, autonomic) · cardiac (AS, arrhythmia – อันตราย)
dehydration / vasodilation→↓venous return→baroreflex ↑sym→“empty ventricle” บีบแรง → mechanoreceptor→paradoxical ↓sym ↑parasym→bradycardia + hypotension = SYNCOPE
Syncope vs seizure: seizure = ชักนาน, lateral tongue biting, postictal confusion, ปวดกล้ามเนื้อหลังชัก; syncope = pale, cold sweat, presyncope (lightheaded, visual dimming)
