Sensitization termsp.60, 75

  • Hyperalgesia = ↑response ต่อ noxious stimulus (เจ็บ 3 → 7)
  • Allodynia = เจ็บจาก non-painful stimulus (ลมพัดก็เจ็บ)
  • Peripheral sensitization = ↑responsiveness/↓threshold ของ peripheral nociceptor
  • Central sensitization = ↑responsiveness ของ central neuron ต่อ normal/subthreshold input (แปลผลเกิน)

Pain modulationp.62, 75

  • Gate control: C fiber ⊣ inhibitory interneuron (เปิด gate) · Aβ fiber (touch) → excite interneuron → ปิด gate (ถู/นวด, TENS)
  • Descending: PAG (endogenous opioids) · nucleus raphe magnus / dorsal raphe (5-HT) · locus coeruleus (NE) → ⊣ 2nd-order neuron ที่ dorsal horn → เหตุผลที่ TCA/SNRI ช่วย chronic pain

Acute migraine drugs (onset ไว + กินเร็ว)p.64–65

  • General: paracetamol (weak) · dopamine antagonist (metoclopramide, prochlorperazine IV in ER – แก้ N/V + migraine; DA excess at hypothalamus) · NSAIDs/COX-2 (ibuprofen, etoricoxib onset เร็ว; celecoxib ช้า) · steroid · opioid (rewarding → ติด, ไม่ค่อยใช้)
  • Specific: triptans (5-HT1B/1D): 1B vessel → vasoconstrict meningeal vessels; 1D trigeminal → ↓CGRP release, ↓transmission to TNC · ditans (5-HT1F, no vasoconstriction) · gepants (CGRP antag)
  • Ergotamine (Cafergot) α1 → peripheral vasoconstriction → ergotism/gangrene; CI vascular dz
  • ให้เร็ว ก่อน central sensitization

Trigeminal neuralgiap.67

  • Unilateral V2/V3 > V1, electric shock-like seconds, trigger zones (สัมผัส เคี้ยว พูด), pain-free between · MRI r/o secondary
  • Rx: carbamazepine 1st line (ตรวจ HLA-B*1502 ก่อน!) · OXC, PHT, lacosamide, gabapentinoids, baclofen · surgery: MVD, gamma knife, rhizotomy

Pathogenesis & clinicalp.17

  • I mucosal colonization → II intravascular survival (capsule, factor H-binding protein; Listeria intracellular → CMI) → III meningeal invasion (transcellular, paracellular, Trojan horse, nerve: Burkholderia) → IV SAS inflammation
  • Classic triad fever + neck stiffness + altered mental status (เพียง 2/3) · headache >85%
  • Kernig/Brudzinski sens. ต่ำ – ไม่มีก็ rule out ไม่ได้
  • N. meningitidis: droplet, close contact → purpura fulminans, DIC ภายในชั่วโมง; chemoprophylaxis contacts
  • Listeria: foodborne/vertical → rhombencephalitis (brainstem), neonatal sepsis, abortion

Investigations & Rxp.17

  • Hemoculture ก่อน antibiotic (50–80% +) · LP (most useful) · CT before LP ถ้า papilledema, focal, ↓LOC, new seizure, immunocompromised
  • Don’t delay antibiotic! (golden period)
  • CSF bacterial: OP >180 mmH₂O, WBC 10–10,000 PMN, glucose <40 mg/dL, CSF/serum <0.4, protein >45, Gram +60%, culture +80%
  • Empirical: ceftriaxone ± vancomycin (+ ampicillin if <1 mo, >50 y, immunocompromised → Listeria)
  • Dexamethasone 0.15 mg/kg q6h × 4 d, ให้ 20 min ก่อน/พร้อม antibiotic dose แรก → ↓mortality & hearing loss (S. pneumoniae adult, Hib child)

CNS INFECTIONS (ต่อ)

CNS infection by syndromep.17, 72

  • Bacterial: acute pyogenic meningitis · chronic (TB) · brain abscess (polymicrobial)
  • Viral: aseptic meningitis (enterovirus) · encephalitis (HSV – temporal lobe, EV71, JE, dengue) · acute flaccid myelitis (polio) · chronic (SSPE, HIV) · post-infectious (GBS)
  • Fungal: Cryptococcus, Candida · Parasitic: Naegleria/Acanthamoeba, Toxoplasma (abscess), cysticercosis

TB meningitisp.17

  • Children <5 y in endemic, adults with HIV; ~50% + miliary TB; BCG 52–100% protective
  • Subacute–chronic (5–30 d): prodrome (low fever, malaise) → meningism, ↓LOC, stroke, hydrocephalus, CN palsy (CN6)
  • CT: basilar arachnoiditis (basal enhancement), tuberculoma, infarct, hydrocephalus
  • Rx: 2HRZE / 10HR (12 mo) + dexamethasone/prednisolone taper 6–8 wk · VP shunt

Brain abscessp.17

  • Routes: contiguous (~50%: ear, mastoid, sinus, dental – single) · hematogenous (~30%: endocarditis, lung, cyanotic CHD R→L (TOF) – multiple, MCA territory, G-W jn) · trauma/surgery (~10%) · cryptogenic 25%
  • Organisms: viridans strep (40%), anaerobes (30%), Enterobacteriaceae, S. aureus · immunocompromised: Nocardia, Toxoplasma, fungi
  • Triad fever + headache + focal deficit (<50%) · stages cerebritis → capsule (~2 wk)
  • NEVER lumbar puncture · CT/MRI contrast: smooth ring, DWI restricted
  • Rx: IV ceftriaxone + metronidazole 6–8 wk + surgery (>2.5 cm, mass effect, near ventricle)

Dementia cases & othersp.17, 69, 76

  • AD: recent/episodic memory → visuospatial, language (hippocampus → parietal)
  • bvFTD: behavior first – disinhibition, apathy, loss of empathy, compulsion (frontal → temporal; tau/TDP-43)
  • NPH: gait apraxia (magnetic) + dementia (psychomotor slow) + urinary incontinence → “potentially reversible” (CSF tap test, VP shunt)
  • TGA: sudden anterograde (+ retrograde) amnesia hours, repetitive questions, resolve <24 h; hippocampus, benign
  • HIE (drowning/arrest): CA1 hippocampus, watershed · Alcoholic cerebellar degeneration: superior vermis → gait ataxia (limbs spared)
  • NTDs: open (myelomeningocele, anencephaly) vs closed; folate, valproate → folic acid
