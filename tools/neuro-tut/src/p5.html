PARKINSON · DOPAMINE PATHWAYS

Parkinsonism vs Parkinson’s diseasep.48–49, 14

  • Parkinsonism (syndrome) = bradykinesia (required) + rigidity / rest tremor / postural instability; masked face, stooped, shuffling, festinating, en-bloc turning, micrographia
  • PD (70%) = degeneration DA neurons SNc; Lewy body (α-synuclein); 2nd most common neurodegenerative
  • Supportive Dx: unilateral onset/asymmetry, rest tremor, levodopa response, levodopa-induced dyskinesia, chronic progressive
  • Atypical parkinsonism (+ red flags): PSP (vertical gaze), MSA (cerebellar, autonomic), DLB, CBD · 2°: drug-induced (antipsychotic), vascular

ANTIPSYCHOTICS · EPS

Typical vs atypical antipsychoticsp.50

  • Typical high potency (haloperidol, fluphenazine, perphenazine, flupenthixol, pimozide): D2 block ที่ dose ต่ำ → EPS, NMS, hyperPRL
  • Typical low potency (chlorpromazine, thioridazine): block H1, M1, α1 ก่อน D2 → sedation, anticholinergic, hypotension; EPS น้อยกว่า
  • Atypical = + 5-HT2A antagonist (↓EPS, ↓negative sx, ↓hyperPRL): SDA (risperidone, paliperidone, lurasidone, ziprasidone) · MARTA (clozapine, olanzapine, quetiapine) · SDRA (amisulpride) · DSS = D2 partial agonist (aripiprazole, brexpiprazole)

Practical usep.50–51

  • Elderly/dementia: avoid H1, M1, α1 & potent D2 → low dose atypical · PD: quetiapine, clozapine
  • Clozapine → agranulocytosis (~2%) → CBC weekly; seizure (>300 mg/d)
  • Black box: antipsychotics ↑ stroke & death in elderly with dementia
  • QTc: avoid IV haloperidol, IV ziprasidone (lowest: aripiprazole, lurasidone) · Metabolic syndrome: olanzapine, clozapine
  • Other uses: antiemetic (prochlorperazine, metoclopramide), intractable hiccup (chlorpromazine), migraine in ER (IV chlorpromazine/prochlorperazine/haloperidol), appetite (olanzapine), chorea/HD

EPS & DA–ACh balancep.51, 75

  • Striatum: DA (D2) ⊣ cholinergic interneuron → block D2 → ACh excess → EPS
  • EPS: acute dystonia → akathisia → parkinsonism → tardive dyskinesia
  • Rx: antimuscarinic (benztropine, trihexyphenidyl, diphenhydramine, dimenhydrinate); akathisia → β-blocker

ANTIDEPRESSANTS · GABA · OPIOIDS

Antidepressantsp.53

  • NE (energy, attention) · 5-HT (mood, appetite, impulse) · DA (drive, reward) · depression ↓5HT (raphe), ↓NE (LC)
  • SSRI 1st line (fluoxetine, sertraline, escitalopram) · SNRI (venlafaxine, duloxetine) · TCA (amitriptyline, nortriptyline: + M1, H1, α1 → anticholinergic, sedation, overdose arrhythmia) · MAO-I · sedating (trazodone, mirtazapine) · multimodal (vortioxetine)

GABA-A & benzodiazepinesp.53–54

  • GABA-A = Cl⁻ channel · BZD bind α–γ = positive allosteric modulator (↑frequency) · Z-drug (zolpidem, α1) · barbiturate (β; ↑duration)
  • Antidote: flumazenil (BZD site; ไม่ reverse barbiturate)
  • Uses: hypnotic, anxiolytic, muscle relaxant, anticonvulsant, alcohol withdrawal
  • ADR: anterograde amnesia, delirium/falls (elderly), complex sleep behavior, RS depression, tolerance, dependence, withdrawal seizure
  • Half-life: short (midazolam, triazolam, zolpidem) · intermediate (alprazolam, lorazepam) · long (clonazepam, clobazam, diazepam)
  • Liver disease → lorazepam (glucuronidation only, no active metabolite) · diazepam/clorazepate สะสม
  • Short-acting: potent, rebound, dependence, withdrawal 1–3 d, amnesia ↑ · long-acting: hangover, withdrawal 4–7 d
Liver-safe BZD “LOT” = Lorazepam · Oxazepam · Temazepam

Opioidsp.55–56

  • Gi-coupled: ↓cAMP, ↓presynaptic Ca²⁺ influx, ↑postsynaptic K⁺ efflux (hyperpolarize) – dorsal horn & brainstem
  • μ: analgesia, resp depression, euphoria, miosis, constipation, dependence · κ: spinal analgesia, dysphoria, diuresis · δ: analgesia
  • Intoxication: coma + respiratory depression + PINPOINT pupils → naloxone (BZD intox: pupils ปกติ)
  • Naloxone 1–2 µg/kg IV q2–3 min, t½ 30 min สั้นกว่า opioid → re-narcotization → infusion
  • Sedation score ≥3 (ปลุกตื่นยาก) = early warning ก่อน RR <10 → hold dose
  • Withdrawal (peak 48–72 h): yawning, lacrimation, rhinorrhea, mydriasis, piloerection, diarrhea, cramps → methadone

ANESTHETICS · ASM · VERTIGO

General & local anestheticsp.56

  • GA components: hypnosis, analgesia, amnesia, immobility (± blunt reflexes) · IV: propofol, ketamine, etomidate, midazolam, thiopental
  • Propofol: ↓CMRO₂, ↓CBF, ↓ICP, anticonvulsant, antiemetic; hypotension, resp depression
  • LA block VG-Na⁺ from inside (unionized crosses membrane) · Amide = 2 “i” (lidocaine, bupivacaine – liver) · Ester = 1 “i” (procaine, tetracaine, cocaine – pseudocholinesterase, PABA allergy)
  • Absorption: IV > tracheal > intercostal > caudal > paracervical > epidural > brachial > sciatic > SC; adrenaline ↓absorption
  • LAST: circumoral numbness, metallic taste, tinnitus → seizure → coma/arrest; bupivacaine most cardiotoxic → 20% lipid emulsion 1.5 mL/kg
Absorption “I Think I Can Promote Every Brachial Sciatic Sub-Q” (IV, Tracheal, Intercostal, Caudal, Paracervical, Epidural, Brachial, Sciatic, SC)

Anti-seizure medicationsp.57–58

  • Na⁺ channel: phenytoin, carbamazepine, oxcarbazepine, lamotrigine, lacosamide (SE: dizzy, ataxia, diplopia) · SV2A: levetiracetam · α2δ: gabapentin, pregabalin · T-type Ca: ethosuximide (absence) · GABA: BZD, barbiturate, tiagabine (GAT-1), vigabatrin (GABA-T) · AMPA: perampanel · Broad: valproate, topiramate
  • Comorbidity: obesity → topiramate, zonisamide (avoid VPA) · depression → lamotrigine (avoid LEV) · migraine → topiramate, VPA · cognitive → LTG, LEV
  • Aromatic ASM (PHT, CBZ, OXC, PHB, LTG) → DRESS/SJS (fever → rash → organ) 1–8 wk · HLA-B*1502 ก่อน CBZ
  • Pregnancy: safest LTG, LEV · highest VPA (NTD) · folic acid ≥0.4 mg/d

Status epilepticus (>5 min)p.58

0–5 min: ABC, DTX, thiamine → 50% glucose, IV→5–20 min BZD: IV diazepam 5–10 mg / IM midazolam 10 / IV lorazepam→20–40 min: IV phenytoin 20 mg/kg, VPA 40, LEV 60 mg/kg, lacosamide, PB→Refractory: anesthetics (midazolam, propofol, thiopental, ketamine) + ICU

Anti-vertigop.58–59

  • Vestibular suppressants (short-term <2 wk; นาน → ขัด compensation): antihistamine (dimenhydrinate, diphenhydramine), Ca-blocker (cinnarizine, flunarizine → drug-induced parkinsonism in elderly), BZD, prochlorperazine, scopolamine
  • Non-suppressant: betahistine (H3 antagonist ≫ H1 weak agonist; ↑inner ear flow; long-term safe), Ginkgo (bleeding), nicergoline/piracetam
