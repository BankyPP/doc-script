VISUAL PATHWAY · PUPIL

Visual field defectsp.20, 14

  • Monocular loss = anterior (pre-chiasmatic: optic n.) · Binocular = chiasm/post-chiasm
  • Chiasm → bitemporal hemianopia: pituitary macroadenoma (snowman), craniopharyngioma
  • Optic tract/LGN → contralateral homonymous hemianopia, incongruous · Occipital → congruous ± macular sparing (PCA)
  • Meyer loop (temporal) = inferior retinal fibers → superior VF: temporal lesion = “pie in the sky”; parietal = “pie on the floor” (sup. fiber ↔ inf. VF สลับกัน)

Pupillary light reflex & RAPDp.22–23

Light→CN2 (afferent)→pretectal n. (both)→EWN→CN3 parasym (efferent)→direct + consensual
  • Lt CN2 lesion: ส่องซ้าย → ไม่หดทั้ง 2 ข้าง; ส่องขวา → หดทั้ง 2 · Swinging light Rt → Lt: Lt pupil dilates = RAPD (Marcus Gunn)
  • RAPD− อาจเป็น CN2 เสียทั้ง 2 ข้างเท่ากันก็ได้
  • Lt CN3 lesion: Lt pupil dilated, ไม่หดไม่ว่าส่องข้างไหน → anisocoria
  • Parinaud: light-near dissociation (pseudo-Argyll Robertson)
LOBE SYNDROMES (ต่อ)

Frontal · agnosia · apraxia · lateralizationp.14

  • Dorsolateral PFC: executive dysfunction, planning, concrete thinking, attention (working memory) · Dorsomedial: apathy, akinetic mutism · Orbitofrontal: emotional lability, disinhibition, perseveration
  • Agnosia = รับรู้แต่แปลความไม่ได้ (visual agnosia, astereognosis, agraphesthesia) – temporo-parieto-occipital association cortex
  • Apraxia = motor planning เสีย: ideomotor/limb-kinetic (premotor) · ideational/conceptual (TPO) · gait apraxia (frontosubcortical: NPH, small vessel)
  • Dominant (L): language, math, complex motor · Non-dominant (R): music, prosody, visuospatial

Rule of 4 (mini-sum)p.26

  • Medial = 4 M: Motor (corticospinal, contra), Medial lemniscus (contra proprioception), MLF, Motor CN nuclei (3, 4, 6, 12)
  • Lateral = 4 S: Spinocerebellar (ipsi ataxia), Spinothalamic (contra pain), Sensory CN5 (ipsi face), Sympathetic (ipsi Horner)
  • CN: 3,4 midbrain · 5–8 pons · 9–12 medulla (4 คู่ต่อชั้น)
