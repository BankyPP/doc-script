ISCHEMIC STROKE – กลไก & ชนิด

Acute stroke overviewp.32–35

  • Ischemic 70% vs hemorrhagic 30% · Clinical = sudden focal deficit อธิบายได้ด้วย vascular territory
  • สาเหตุ: disease of blood vessel · cardioembolism · blood component

Large vessel atherosclerosis

  • ① Plaque rupture → platelet thrombus → occlusion หรือ artery-to-artery embolism (หลุดไปอุดปลาย)
  • ② Fixed stenosis → low flow (demand–supply mismatch) → watershed

Small vessel disease → lacunar infarct

  • Perforator occlusion (lipohyalinosis จาก HT) · lesion ≤15 mm: basal ganglia, internal capsule, thalamus, pons, cerebellum (ตำแหน่งเดียวกับ hypertensive ICH)

Penumbra · Time is brainp.35

  • Ischemic core (ตายแล้ว) + penumbra (salvageable) → ยิ่งช้า core ยิ่งโต
  • 1 min = 1.9 million neurons & 14 billion synapses → stroke fast track

Global ischemia & watershedp.34, 44

  • Hypotension, shock, cardiac arrest → transient = syncope · prolonged = anoxic brain injury
  • Selective vulnerability: CA1 hippocampus (memory), cerebellar Purkinje, medium striatal neurons, cortical pyramidal layers 3, 5, 6
  • Watershed (border zone) ACA–MCA, MCA–PCA → “man in a barrel” (bilateral proximal arm weakness), transcortical aphasia
Hypoxia-sensitive: “Hippo Purks in the Stripes” = Hippocampus CA1 · Purkinje · Striatum · pyramidal layers 3,5,6

Etiology & TOASTp.36

  • Heart: structural (VHD, MI, cardiomyopathy, PFO paradoxical) · electrical (AF) · other emboli (fat, air, tumor, septic)
  • Vessel: atherosclerosis (extra/intracranial) · non-athero: vasculitis, dissection, RCVS, small vessel
  • Blood: cells, coagulation
TOAST “CLOSU”: Cardioembolic · Large artery athero · Other determined · Small vessel (lacunar) · Undetermined (cryptogenic)

SUM: Acute stroke algorithm (ลายมือ)p.36–38

Suspected stroke (sudden focal deficit) → confirm onset, rule out mimics (hypoglycemia, seizure, migraine) ↓ CT brain non-contrast → แยก ischemic vs hemorrhagic (manage ต่างกัน!!) ↓ ischemic
≤ 4.5 h → IV alteplase (rtPA) / tenecteplase
ก่อนให้ BP <185/110 (เกิน = ห้าม, ลดก่อน)
หลังให้ keep <180/105 ≤ 24 h (large vessel, CTA/perfusion) → mechanical thrombectomy (ลากลิ่มเลือดออก)
Not candidate: BP <220/120 → ไม่ต้องลด (permissive HT เลี้ยง penumbra) · ≥220/120 → ลด ~15% ใน 24 h

NEUROIMAGING PATTERNS (อื่น ๆ)

Imaging pearlsp.46

  • Hepatic encephalopathy: bilateral T1 hyperintense globus pallidus (manganese)
  • Radiation/chemo: symmetric periventricular WM T2 hyperintensity
  • AD: temporal/parietal atrophy, disproportionate hippocampal loss; FDG-PET hypometabolism
  • NPH: ventriculomegaly > atrophy, DESH; triad wet, wobbly, wacky
  • Meningitis: leptomeningeal enhancement · TB: basal cisterns enhancement + hydrocephalus + infarct
  • Abscess: early cerebritis → late cerebritis → early capsule (~2 wk) → late capsule; smooth ring, DWI restricted
  • Sturge-Weber: tram-track calcification + atrophy · Schizencephaly: cleft lined by gray matter
HEMORRHAGIC STROKE · ICP · HERNIATION

Spontaneous ICH & SAHp.41–42

  • Hypertensive ICH: HT vasculopathy (เหมือน lacunar) → putamen/BG-IC, thalamus, pons, cerebellum · Rx tight BP (nicardipine, labetalol), surgery if indicated
  • Other: vascular malformation, cerebral amyloid angiopathy (lobar, elderly)
  • SAH: ruptured berry aneurysm (circle of Willis: ACoA, PCoA, MCA bifurcation) → thunderclap headache; CT −ve → LP: pink/xanthochromic CSF · Rx clipping / coil

CBF autoregulation · Monro–Kellie · CPPp.42–43

  • CBF ~50 mL/100 g/min คงที่เมื่อ MAP/CPP 50–150 mmHg; นอกช่วง → passive
  • Brain + Blood + CSF = constant (+ mass) → compensate (CSF ออก, vein collapse) → เกิน = ICP ↑
  • CPP = MAP − ICP (− JVP) → ICP ↑ → CPP ↓ → CBF ↓ → ischemia
  • ICP monitoring: epidural, subdural, intraparenchymal fiberoptic, ventriculostomy (วัด + drain CSF ได้)

↑ICP: causes · S&S · Rx by causep.43

  • Causes: ↑brain (edema) · ↑CSF (hydrocephalus) · ↑blood (hyperemia, hypercapnia) · ↑other (tumor, hematoma – most common)
  • S&S: headache (ตอนเช้า, Valsalva), vomiting, blurred vision, papilledema, CN6 palsy (most common CN), Cushing triad: HT + bradycardia + irregular respiration; infant: หัวโต, tense fontanelle, setting-sun eyes

Canadian CT head rule (GCS 13–15 + LOC/amnesia)p.46

  • High risk: GCS <15 at 2 h · suspected open/depressed fx · sign basilar skull fx (hemotympanum, raccoon eyes, Battle sign, CSF oto/rhinorrhea) · vomiting ≥2 · age ≥65
  • Medium: retrograde amnesia ≥30 min · dangerous mechanism (pedestrian struck, ejected, fall >3 ft / 5 stairs)

Metastasis & clinical featuresp.46

  • Mets: multiple at gray–white junction (end artery), vasogenic edema; primary: lung (most), breast, melanoma, renal, colorectal
  • Mass (solid, ผ่าตัดได้) vs infiltrative (lymphoma, GBM → chemo/RT)
  • Clinical: direct deficit (tumor + edema), headache (traction), seizure (cortical irritation), false localizing (CN6), infarction, tumor bleeding · course progressive (malignant wks–mo; benign yrs) แต่อาจ sudden
  • ↑ICP in tumor: mass effect, obstructive hydrocephalus, venous sinus occlusion · headache เช้า (CO₂ retention), Valsalva
  • Cavernous sinus tumor: diplopia (3,4,6), V1–V2 numbness, chemosis, orbital pain
  • Chemo: area postrema → vomiting; length-dependent axonopathy (cisplatin, taxanes, vinca) · Radiation (delayed): necrosis, cranial neuropathy
